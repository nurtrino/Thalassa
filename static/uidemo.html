Intro rules

The Voyage

  1. Roll the bronze die — one to three — and sail the lanes.
  2. Four passes breach the mountain wall; each realm runs seven isles deep.
  3. Trials arm your blows. Strike and magic are questions; a miss can backfire.
  4. Bosses swing heavy every third round — guard to turn the whole blow aside.
  5. Planks patch the hull. Slay each tyrant, carry its relic home, bank all four.
